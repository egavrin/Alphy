The card must ask for age, measurement method, measurement time, symptoms, feeding, and urine output.
These are required observations, not facts supplied by the original request.
A value of 37.5°C alone does not establish a fever diagnosis or exclude serious illness.
NHS guidance defines a high temperature as 38°C or more. [NHS fever guidance](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/fever-in-children/)

NICE classifies the younger group as high risk and the 3–6-month group as at least intermediate risk.
The NHS public guidance supplies the urgent-action wording above.
