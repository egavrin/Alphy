# One Scene Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Stages 1 to 3 of the design (its section 15). Each stage is a finished component with its own push to `main` and its own release:

1. "A clean stage and an exact voice" (Alpha 18).
2. "The scene speaks and walks" (Alpha 19).
3. "Every turn updates the stage" (Alpha 20).

**Architecture:**

- Stage 1 marks each text of Alphy for exact speech. It gives the stage one surface and the polish of design section 13. The state line gets the value of each control.
- Stage 2 lets the template call write narration lines at the end of the template file. The stage turns on the controls before these lines arrive. A narrator in the voice bridge sends one marked sentence ahead and moves the stage when the speaker plays the start of that sentence.
- Stage 3 adds a follow call in its own lane. It decides what the stage does after a voice-only turn, and the chat keeps that turn as a spoken pair.

**Tech Stack:** Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Android instrumented tests (AndroidJUnitRunner), the GPT-Live socket of OpenAI, and the kit in JavaScript with Bun 1.3.9.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-22-voice-scene-and-forms-design.md` (the design). Its evidence:

- `evidence/ux-2026-11-21/code-facts.md` (CF) and `evidence/ux-2026-11-21/lane-b.md`.

The `evidence/` directory is not in Git. It exists only in the worktree `app-file-parts`.

**Base:** branch `worktree-app-file-parts` at `8fb84bb` (Alpha 17).

## Review Focus

These five failure modes are the most likely to reach a person, because no simple test meets them by chance.

1. **The watcher reads a narrator press as a tap, and the walk stops itself** (stage 2). Expected: a press by the narrator or by a voice turn enters the state line with its own `who` and stops nothing. Pinned by `aNarratorPressDoesNotStopTheWalk` (Task 31) and `eachHostPressHasItsWho` (Task 29).
2. **The host saves late narration over a scene that changed or left the stage** (stage 2). Expected: the later scene stays as it is, and the narration goes only into the file that its call wrote. Pinned by `lateNarrationKeepsALaterScene` (Task 27).
3. **The voice speaks some words and then hands off in the same turn** (stage 3). Expected: one user message, no spoken pair in the chat, and no follow call that still runs. Pinned by `wordsThenAHandOffLeaveOneUserMessage` (Task 44).
4. **A talk in a language other than Russian** (stage 1). Expected: a marked text holds only the text of Alphy in the language of the request. No Russian host word is under the mark. Pinned by `noHostWordIsUnderTheMark` (Task 3).
5. **A sentence that waits at the voice model plays after the walk stops** (stage 2). Expected: the stage does not move at its start, and the host drops its audio. Pinned by `aStoppedWalkMovesNoStep` (Task 31).

## Decisions of this plan

The design leaves these choices to the plan (design section 18). Each item has its label and its reason. Where the design and the code conflict, the design wins.

1. **The split of the template code** (Implementation choice). New flat modules, in this order in `MODULES` of `kit/build.js`: `…, "change", "form-base", "form-scene", "form-items", "form-cells", "forms", "template", "compile"`.
   - `form-base.js` holds what each form uses: `BUDGET`, `COLOR_NAMES`, `TEMPLATE_HEAD`, `SENTENCE_END`, `cut`, `cutLong`, `textFault`, `headFault`, `colored`, `WIDTH`, `HEIGHT`, and `INSIDE`.
   - Each form module exports one `TemplateForm` object. `forms.js` exports `FORMS_BY_WORD`.
   - `template.js` keeps the read, the merge, the expansion, and the build. It re-exports `BUDGET`, `TEMPLATE_HEAD`, and `isTemplateFile`, so its importers stay unchanged.
   - Reason: a data stage (4 or 5 of the design) adds a form module and one line of `forms.js`. After Task 2, this plan changes neither `forms.js` nor `kit/build.js`, so the data stages and this plan share no template source file (design section 15, and the section "The data stages beside stages 2 and 3").
2. **Narration lines** (Requirement: design 8.1. The forms are Implementation choices).
   - A `steps` control takes `say1 = "…"` to `sayN = "…"`, one for each step. A slider, a choice, or no control takes one `say = "…"`.
   - Reason for the choice: design 8.1 names only stepped forms and sliders. A choice has no walk, so it takes one sentence of what to try.
   - Rank 4, after the card. The merge keeps the narration lines last.
   - The cap is `BUDGET.say = 110`, cut by `cut(text, 110)`.
   - The build leaves out a narration line with no sentence end inside 110 characters. It also leaves out a narration line of the wrong kind: `say` beside `steps`, `sayK` beside a slider or a choice, or `sayK` above the count of steps.
   - A left-out narration line adds no fault to the compile faults, so no repair call follows. Reason: a missing line is valid (design 8.1), and a repair call costs a call for a line that draws nothing.
   - `kept` writes each narration line as `sayK = "<JSON string>"`, so the host reads it with `JSONArray("[" + literal + "]")`.
   - This decides DR §2 "one `say` line for each option index, or a fourth item argument": one `sayK` line for each step index.
3. **The steps control** (Implementation choice).
   - `scene`: `control = steps(LABEL, [STEP, …], ANSWER)`. `items`: `control = steps(LABEL, ANSWER)`, and the item labels are the steps.
   - ANSWER is optional. Its default is the last step (design 9.2).
   - Caps: LABEL `BUDGET.control`, each STEP `BUDGET.option`, 2 to `BUDGET.options` steps.
   - It expands to `input("choice", LABEL, ANSWER, options=[…])`, the native segmented control of a choice. The start is the answer step.
   - The host knows a stepped scene by `^control\s*=\s*steps\(` in the stored template file.
4. **The template prompt** (Requirement: design 8.1, 9.2). All changes are in `## Template`, so `TEMPLATE_HASH` changes and `PROMPT_HASH` stays `69759b168bc3`.
   - Line order: the first line, the control, the parts, the card, then the narration.
   - A sequence gets `steps` and one `sayK` for each step. A `sayK` says what the picture shows at step K.
   - A slider or a choice gets one `say`: what to move and what to watch.
   - The first state shows the point: the answer step, or a slider start that shows it.
   - The rule "the voice gives the steps" goes away.
   - The examples get narration lines.
5. **The opener** (Requirement: design 8.2. Stage 2, Task 28). The screen rule of `voiceScreenInstructions` becomes one short spoken sentence with the short answer, no parts, no steps, and no control name. The template call of a later screen still gets the routing text, with no change. Stage 1 keeps the present routing text for a screen (the owner's word: without narration, a one-sentence opener makes an answer thinner).
6. **Controls before narration** (Requirement: design 14. The mechanism is an Implementation choice).
   - For a new screen, the stream compiles the complete lines before the first narration line when that line starts. A clean compile gives the scene outcome at once, with a `narration` deferred value. The early compile runs only when those lines hold the card. A narration line that comes before the card gives no early compile, and the present path runs at the end of the call.
   - The host stores the scene and turns the controls on. The call goes on.
   - At the end of the call, the host saves the whole kept text with `ToolStore.saveTool(record, revision = false)`. It saves only when the stored tool still holds the same app file at the same revision. Otherwise it drops the narration.
   - The rest of the call runs in the supervisor scope of `ToolController` (`scope`, the `viewModelScope` of the shell) with its own `CloudSession`. `startEarlyPlan` already runs the early template call in this form (`tools/ToolController.kt:495`). A screen run with no early call gets the same form.
   - The mode end, `cancelRequest` of its message, and a deletion of the tool cancel that session. The next request has its own session, so the queue does not wait for the narration.
   - When the early compile is not clean, the present path runs at the end of the call. A change of a scene uses the present path.
7. **Exact speech** (Requirement: design 8.3. The text split is an Implementation choice).
   - `LiveProtocol.MARK = "Дословно: "`.
   - Each append that carries a text of Alphy holds the mark, and only the text of Alphy follows the mark. Stage 1 covers the answer, the ready screen, the screen start, and the typed turn. Stage 2 adds the narrator and the tap sentence with the same mark.
   - A host line with no text of Alphy goes without the mark. The voice says it in the language of the talk.
   - The approval lines (`PROPOSE`, `ASK`, `STARTED`, `CANCELLED`, `DONE`, `NOT_DONE`) stay one unmarked line, as today.
   - Reason: no Russian host word goes under the mark in a talk of another language (Review Focus 4). The approval keeps its behavior and its tests (design 8.3).
   - The instruction line says "the text after the mark", not "a text that starts with the mark". The typed turn puts the typed words before the mark. Task 15 confirms this form, because the probe tested only a mark at the start.
8. **The host lines** (Requirement: design 8.3. The text is an Implementation choice). The form of address is «ты». The approval lines change their wording, and their behavior and their assertions stay. Tests that copy a line as a literal switch to the constant. The new copy is in Task 3.
9. **The state line** (Implementation choice). `ShownScreen` JSON gets three fields:
   - `values` (stage 1, Task 4): `[{"control": LABEL, "value": V}]`, one for each bound input in the order of the UI, sliders included.
   - `step` (stage 2, Task 28): `{"index": K, "count": N, "label": OPTION}`, only for a stepped scene. K starts at 1.
   - `actions` (stage 2, Task 28): at most 3 entries, oldest first, `[{"who": "tap"|"voice"|"narrator", "control": LABEL, "value": V}]`.
   - At the 8000 cap, the app file goes first, then the controls. These fields always stay.
10. **Who did an action** (Implementation choice, stage 2). The bridge watches the stage state with `snapshotFlow` (DR §8, choice b).
    - Before each press of its own, the bridge sets the expected change: control, value, and who. The watcher gives the next change of that control that `who`.
    - A routing press and «Дальше» are `voice`. A follow press of stage 3 is `voice` too. A walk press is `narrator`. Each other change is `tap`.
    - Changes of one control by `tap` less than 1 s apart replace the last action. So a slider drag is one action.
    - A new stage tool resets the watched values and adds no action.
11. **A tap is heard** (Requirement: design 8.5, stage 2). A tap on a step while the session does not speak sends `thinking(MARK + sentence)`. A tap while it speaks sends nothing. A tap on a slider or a choice sends nothing. A tap stops a walk.
12. **The session members** (Implementation choice, stage 2). `LiveVoiceHost` gets `outputText(text, startMs, endMs, replyStart, arrivedNanos)` and `inputStarted()`. `LiveVoiceSession` gets `onsetAfter(armedNanos, fire)`, `cancelOnset()`, and `skip(sentence)`. `LiveAudio` gets `playedFrames()`.
13. **The sync rule at the playback moment** (Requirement: design 7.2, as written).
    - The arm is the arrival time of the first transcript piece of a sentence. Received audio has frame positions in the order of arrival.
    - The target is the first voiced chunk received after the arm with at least 200 ms of silence before it.
    - Silence is silent received frames, or a gap in arrival with no chunks. Both forms count: a queued sentence can come as a new reply or inside the reply before it (PR section 3: one of three queued pairs merged). Task 33 records which form GPT-Live sends.
    - With no such chunk received within 1.5 s of the arm, the target is the frame received 0.45 s after the arm.
    - The change fires when `playedFrames()` reaches the target, polled every 20 ms. When the speaker already played the target, it fires at once.
    - Reason: the playback buffer holds up to 5 s (`voice/LiveAudio.kt`), so a received frame usually plays later than it arrives. The probe emulator played no sound (PR section "Limits").
14. **The narrator** (Requirement: design 9.1, 9.2. The class is an Implementation choice). A new class `ui/StageNarrator.kt`, owned by the bridge.
    - A walk starts by itself only for a new stepped scene of the present request, when its template call ends. At that time the host has saved or dropped the narration.
    - The scene stores with the answer step, so the answer shows during the opener. The narrator sends sentence 1 at once, and it waits behind the opener.
    - Step K arms at the first transcript piece, after the send of sentence K, whose text starts sentence K. The narrator keeps the text since the end of the last sentence for this match. A reply start is not necessary, because a queued sentence can come inside the reply before it (PR section 3). The narrator ignores a text that does not match, such as the opener or a wait line.
    - At the sync fire of sentence K, the narrator presses step K and sends sentence K+1. So at most one sentence waits.
    - The card text of step K replaces a missing `sayK` (design 8.1). That text exists only after the press, so step K is sequential: at the reply end of the sentence before it, the narrator presses step K, then sends `MARK + card text`.
    - The walk stops on `inputStarted`, a tap on the scene, a new hand-off, or a new screen. It never starts again by itself.
    - A new scene with one `say` (a slider, a choice, or no control) gets no walk. The narrator sends `MARK + say` once, when the host saves the narration, and it waits behind the opener. A follow screen and an old scene say nothing. Reason: this sentence is the only spoken answer to "what do I press" (design 8.1).
15. **A stopped walk** (Requirement: design 8.5, 9.1). The sentence that plays ends. For a waiting sentence, the narrator calls `skip`. From the first transcript piece that starts that sentence, the session drops its transcript pieces until the reply ends. From the next voiced start by the sync rule, it also drops the audio. This holds also when the sentence comes inside the reply before it. It sends no new event to the voice model: the wire protocol has no event that cancels a reply (`voice/LiveProtocol.kt`), and an error answer to an unknown event ends the voice mode (`voice/LiveVoiceSession.kt:240`). A skip acts once, so a later tap or «Дальше» on that step still gets its sentence. After a spoken word, the narrator calls `skip` only when Task 24 finds branch (b).
16. **«Дальше» and «Продолжай»** (Requirement: design 9.1).
    - «Дальше» is a routing press of the next step label. For a press of the stepped control, the bridge says `MARK + sayK` and not the routing text.
    - «Продолжай» is a press of a host control with the label of `R.string.stage_continue_steps` («Продолжить по шагам»). `ShownScreen` lists it only for a stepped scene that is not at its last step. Its press walks the rest.
17. **The strip and the approval window in a walk** (Implementation choice). Between two sentences of a walk, the strip keeps the speaking status, and `outputEnded` changes no approval window. Reason: the gap is 0.6 to 0.9 s (PR Q1 c), and a flicker to the listening status invites speech in the middle of a walk.
18. **The follow call** (Requirement: design 10.2. The schema is an Implementation choice).
    - `CloudRequest.follow: Boolean = false`. With it, the routing body offers one strict tool `submit_follow_decision`.
    - The schema has `kind` (`none`, `screen`, `mark`, `press`, `clear`), `brief`, and `target`. All three are required.
    - The provider emits `CloudEvent.Follow(kind, brief, target)`. `FollowDecision.decode` gives `None` for a target that is not in the controls list.
    - In this plan, `mark` and `press` both select the named option, quietly. The trace records them apart.
19. **The follow signal** (Requirement: design 10.2). At `outputText` with `replyStart`, the bridge starts a follow call when these three conditions hold:
    - A person turn came.
    - No hand-off came after that turn.
    - The host sent no text after that turn.

    A new turn or a hand-off cancels the follow call. A screen build of a follow call stops too.
20. **Saved voice-only turns** (Requirement: design 10.3. This decides DR §9 "save in the chat or keep in memory": in the chat).
    - `Message.spoken: Boolean = false`, in the JSON of the conversation.
    - At the reply start, the bridge makes the pair in memory. The words of the voice append to it.
    - The pair persists at `outputEnded`, at the next turn, at the end of the mode, or when a follow screen needs it.
    - A hand-off in the same turn drops the pair before it persists (Review Focus 3).
    - `spokenContext` reads a persisted pair with no change.
21. **Stage lifetime** (Requirement: design 10.4. The triggers are Implementation choices).
    - A follow `clear` sets `stageToolId = null`. The scene stays in the chat.
    - A waiting plan card leaves the stage at a follow `clear` or `screen`, or at a later completed hand-off that is not its spoken approval. Limit: this plan has no model signal for "a different subject". The live check counts G4.
    - A tracked hand-off that searched and ended with no app clears `stageToolId`.
    - A work card replaces the stage app while a tracked hand-off searches, or builds with no frame yet. Its texts are «Ищу: %s» and «Собираю экран: %s». The subject is the words of the hand-off, cut at a word end at 48 characters.
    - Precedence: a change of the scene on the stage dims that scene (decision 22). A build of a new screen shows the work card.
22. **Polish** (Requirement: design 13. The mechanisms are Implementation choices).
    - One surface: `NativePreset` gets `stage: Boolean`. On the stage it paints no `colors.background`, and `LocalToolBackground` is the shell surface (DR §12). The full mini-app on the stage gets the same flag.
    - The mascot is the same picture with an alpha channel. A flood fill from the four corners clears `#FCF5EA`.
    - `commandsOf` in `kit/src/draw.js` gets a point-in-polygon test, so a label on a polygon takes the contrast of the polygon fill.
    - A new part scrolls into view with a stage `ScrollState` and a `BringIntoViewRequester`. It gets an accent mark for 1 s.
    - During a change, the old scene shows at alpha 0.45 with its controls off, and the frames of the change do not mix with it. The design wins over Task 18 of Alpha 17 (the change preview over the stored scene).
    - The slider preview shows no input warning and no «Применить».
    - A stage error shows a fixed text for its error group in the app locale, with what the person can say next.
23. **O1** (Requirement: design 13). Task 13 finds the cause first. Task 14 has one fix for reading A and one fix for reading B. The finding selects the fix. Both apply when both readings hold.
24. **Owner's point 3** (Requirement: design 8.5). The phone check of Task 5 selects Task 6 or Task 7. The other task does not run.
